when life gets in the way
Travel, holidays, social weeks
Drop back to 16:8 only — no extended fasts. Even a 12-hour overnight fast (finish dinner by 8pm, don't eat until 8am) maintains the baseline metabolic rhythm. Skip a week of 24h fasting without guilt; consistency over months matters more than any single week.
If you feel run down or sick
Cancel the extended fast that week, no negotiation. Your immune system needs fuel when fighting something. Resume the 24h fast the following week when you feel well.
If Justin has jujutsu training
His Wednesday adjustment is permanent: fast overnight, eat at 8am post-training, eat normally (including lunch) through the day until 8pm. He skips the extended 24h fast on training mornings. Same pattern applies any week he trains on a fast day.

These daily habits compound the fasting benefits on every day — not just fast days. Once they're automatic they require no willpower, and they meaningfully extend the anti-inflammatory and apoptotic effects of the fasting protocol.

morning
16oz water with a pinch of salt on waking
Rehydrates after overnight fasting, replaces sodium lost through fasting-related urination, and reduces morning hunger. Do it before coffee — it takes 10 seconds and makes a real difference on fast days.
Black coffee or green tea before noon
Both extend autophagy signaling into the morning and blunt hunger during the fast window. Green tea's EGCG is independently anti-inflammatory. Keep it black — milk or sweeteners break the fast.
10–20 min fasted walk after waking
Fasted morning movement amplifies AMPK activation and extends fat-burning. Doesn't need to be intense — a neighborhood walk is enough. Also dramatically improves mood and focus for the rest of the morning.
eating window
Omega-3s 3–4× per week
Fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines), walnuts, or flaxseed. Omega-3s directly suppress NFκB — one of the main inflammatory transcription factors. This is the single highest-impact dietary habit for inflammation.
Polyphenols daily
Berries, dark chocolate (70%+), green tea, olive oil, turmeric, red onion. These directly modulate Bcl-2/Bax ratios and support healthy apoptosis even on non-fasting days. Aim for color variety on the plate.
Fermented food daily
Greek yogurt, kimchi, kefir, miso, or sauerkraut. Gut microbiome diversity directly influences systemic inflammation levels. One serving a day makes a measurable difference in 4–6 weeks.
Stop eating 2–3h before bed
Late eating blunts growth hormone release during sleep and disrupts circadian signaling. If dinner is at 7pm and the eating window opens at noon, that's already 17 hours fasted. The window practically closes itself.
evening & general
7–8 hours sleep, consistent bedtime
Growth hormone peaks in deep sleep and directly complements apoptotic cellular cleanup. Inconsistent schedules disrupt this even if total hours are fine. Same bedtime ±30 min makes a real measurable difference.
Resistance training 2–3× per week
Muscle contraction independently triggers AMPK and promotes healthy apoptosis in damaged muscle cells. Bodyweight training counts (push-ups, squats, lunges). Schedule on non-extended-fast days.
2–3L water daily
Especially important during extended fasts. Add electrolytes (pinch of salt, or a small cup of bone broth) on 24h and 36h fast days — you're flushing more sodium than usual and most fasting headaches are actually dehydration or electrolyte depletion.

How to build from the 9-day kickstart into a sustainable long-term practice. Each phase has a different goal — don't rush the progression. Consistency over 12 weeks beats intensity for 3 weeks then stopping.

1
Weeks 1–2 — consolidate
Goal: make 16:8 automatic · You are here
Finish the 9-day kickstart. Start the maintenance rhythm: 16:8 weekdays, one 24h fast on Wednesday, free weekends. Don't try to be perfect — just keep the window. The main adaptation is your body stopping the cortisol spike it uses to raise blood sugar in the morning. Once that settles (~10–14 days), morning hunger disappears.

2
Weeks 3–4 — deepen
Goal: optimize the eating window
16:8 should feel natural by now. Start paying more attention to what's inside the window — shift toward anti-inflammatory foods, reduce refined sugar and ultra-processed snacks. Try pushing 2 weekdays to 18:6. Add the monthly 36h fast if feeling strong and well-rested.

3
Weeks 5–8 — find the rhythm
Goal: sustainable long-term pattern
This is where compounding starts. The weekly 24h fast keeps mTOR suppressed in a meaningful rhythm. You should notice inflammation markers subjectively — less joint stiffness, better skin, more consistent energy. Add resistance training if not already doing it. The monthly 36h fast is now the deeper reset.

4
Weeks 9–12 — evaluate and adjust
Goal: tune for your life
By now you'll have a clear sense of what's working. Some people thrive with just the weekly 24h fast and never need more. Others prefer two shorter extended fasts. If weight loss has plateaued and that matters, tighten the eating window 1–2 days per week. If energy is low, add more food in the window — not more fasting.

honest expectations
Weight
0.5–1kg per month steadily, more in the first 2 weeks (mostly water from glycogen depletion). Don't weigh daily — weekly at the same time of day is more meaningful. The scale moving slowly is the sustainable version.
Inflammation
Subjective improvements (energy, skin, joint comfort) often show within 3–4 weeks. Blood markers (CRP, IL-6) take 8–12 weeks of consistency to shift meaningfully — worth asking your doctor for a panel at the 3-month mark.
Cellular health and apoptosis
This is a long game — the benefits accumulate over months and years, not weeks. Think of the weekly 24h fast as maintenance for your cells, not a quick fix. The evidence is strongest at the 6–12 month mark.
The main thing
80% consistency over 12 weeks beats 100% for 3 weeks then stopping. Build the rhythm, not the streak. A missed fast day doesn't undo anything — just pick up the next window and keep going.
For general wellness. Consult your doctor before beginning if you have a medical condition.
