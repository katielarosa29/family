Junior Ranger Badges + Cozy Thanksgiving

Canyon Coaster or Kaibab Lake, Fly Home
  • Morning: Canyon Coaster if missed, or Kaibab Lake walk
  • Drive Phoenix (~2.5 hrs)
  • Fly home PDX
  • Note: Joey's soccer tournament is Nov 29 (2 days after return)

Alternative / Future Trip · ~7 Days
PCH Camper Van
Road Trip
💭 Option for Future 🚐 Portland → Carmel

Seven days driving the California coast in a camper van — Portland to Carmel and back. Redwoods, Bodega Bay vintage shops, Point Reyes, Santa Cruz skate park for Kit, and a Thanksgiving beach picnic in Carmel. This was the other Thanksgiving contender before Grand Canyon won.

Duration
7 days

Route & Stops
Day 1 — Portland → Eugene/Roseburg Area
Departure Day
  • Pick up Cruise America Class C van in Portland
  • McMenamins Edgefield (Troutdale) — fueling stop, walk the grounds
  • Drive south through Willamette Valley
  • Camp Eugene/Roseburg area
Day 2 — Redwoods
Into California, Big Trees
  • Cross into California — Crescent City
  • Prairie Creek Redwoods State Park — Fern Canyon hike (like Jurassic Park)
  • Avenue of the Giants drive (tallest trees on earth)
  • Camp in or near Humboldt Redwoods
Day 3 — Bodega Bay + Point Reyes
Coastal Vibes, Vintage Finds
  • Bodega Bay — Eclectic Amanda vintage shop
  • Oysters at The Tides Wharf if mood strikes
  • Point Reyes National Seashore — Elephant seal overlook (fall migration!)
  • Drakes Beach or Limantour Beach walk
  • Camp at Point Reyes or continue toward SF
Day 4 — San Francisco Day
City Stop
  • Cross Golden Gate into the city
  • Park van at RV-friendly lot, explore by transit/foot
  • Mission District tacos (Kit-friendly), Dolores Park
  • Ferry Building — grab provisions for the road
  • Continue south toward Santa Cruz
Day 5 — Santa Cruz
Skate Park for Kit, Bakery Stop
  • Santa Cruz Skate Park — Kit's highlight
  • Gayle's Bakery (Capitola) — legendary pastries
  • Santa Cruz Wharf for lunch
  • Continue south on PCH toward Carmel
  • Camp at Pfeiffer Big Sur State Park
Day 6 — Carmel (Thanksgiving)
Beach Picnic Thanksgiving
  • Carmel-by-the-Sea — white sand beach Thanksgiving picnic
  • Cannery Row Antiques Mall (Katie's pick)
  • 17-Mile Drive — Lone Cypress, Pebble Beach views
  • Whole Foods Carmel or Monterey for picnic provisions
  • Head north for the return leg
Day 7 — Return via Ashland
MÄS Restaurant, Home
  • Drive north via Hwy 101 / I-5
  • Ashland, OR stop — MÄS restaurant for dinner
  • Wake early, drive Ashland → Portland (~5 hrs), home by noon
  • Return RV after arrival

Ski Trip
📋 Being Planned ⛷️ Trailborn Jackson Hole

New Year's ski trip to Jackson Hole — all four LaRosas on green runs, Trailborn Jackson Hole (Marriott Bonvoy), and the iconic New Year's Eve torchlight parade and fireworks on the mountain. Joey and Kit are green run skiers with room to grow.

Jackson Hole Hotel Options (Bonvoy)
Trailborn Jackson Hole (Bonvoy)
  • Marriott Outdoor Collection — earns Bonvoy points
  • Three rate tiers seen: Prepay Non-refundable ($483 avg), Member Flexible ($535 avg), Member Exclusive ($560 avg + 3k bonus pts)
  • All include $50/night resort fee
  • Free cancellation on Flexible/Exclusive before Dec 24
  • 6 nights = ~$2,900–3,363 per room
Points Strategy
  • ~175k Bonvoy points on hand
  • Could use Delta miles for flights (200k miles banked)
  • Potential savings of $3,250–5,900 using points
  • New Year's week = peak pricing, book early
  • Marriott Boundless card earns 6.26x at Bonvoy hotels
Day-by-Day Itinerary
Dec 27 (Sunday)
Arrive Jackson
  • Fly PDX → JAC (or SLC + drive ~4 hrs)
  • Check in Trailborn, get settled
  • Rent ski gear if needed — book ahead in peak week
  • Jackson Town Square — elk antler arches, dinner
Dec 28–30 (Mon–Wed)
Ski Days at JHMR

  • Kids ski school option if Joey or Kit want instruction

  • Downtown Jackson for shopping + dinner evenings
Dec 31 (Thursday)
New Year's Eve — Torchlight Parade
  • Ski during the day
  • JHMR New Year's Eve Torchlight Parade at dusk — skiers with torches down the mountain
  • Fireworks over the resort — a major highlight for the kids
  • Celebratory dinner — reserve well ahead
Jan 1 (Friday)
New Year's Day — Rest or Ski

  • Grand Teton National Park drive if road is clear

Fly Home
