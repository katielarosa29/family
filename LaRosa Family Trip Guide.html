Redwoods to Mendocino
Camper Van Trip
📋 Being Planned 🚐 20.5 ft Cruise America van

Six nights down the Northern California coast and back: Oregon cheese country, Fern Canyon and elk at Gold Bluffs, oysters and Victorian Ferndale, a night on the Lost Coast, the biggest redwoods on earth, and a Thanksgiving picnic on the Mendocino Headlands. Short driving days, 7am starts, and every afternoon free to explore.

Thanksgiving Menu — Simple & Local

To Start

  • Humboldt Bay oysters (from Eureka Tuesday) — grilled with garlic-herb butter until they pop open, plus a few raw with lemon for the grown-ups
  • Cheese board: Rogue River Blue (Oregon), Humboldt Fog (Arcata), Rumiano cheddar for the kids, with local bread and apples

The Feast

  • Turkey breast or tenderloin on the grill (or a deli-roasted breast, warmed in foil)
  • Sweet potatoes roasted in foil in the coals
  • Cranberry sauce, green salad, warm crusty bread & butter
  • Fresh local fish from Noyo Harbor if the boats are in

Dessert

  • A pie from a Mendocino bakery (buy Wednesday)
  • Grilled apples with a little blue cheese and honey
  • Hot cocoa and s'mores by the fire

Shop ahead: cheese Sun & Mon, oysters + bread Tue, everything else Wed — most shops close Thursday. Live oysters keep a few days on ice, cup side down.

No local Dungeness crab, probably: the Northern California commercial season normally can't open before December and has been delayed in recent years.

Fires: At California state parks, wood fires usually have to stay in the provided fire ring, so plan to use the Takibi with charcoal for grilling and ask each campground before lighting wood in it. Check for any burn restrictions that week.

Firewood: Don't bring firewood from Oregon. Buy it where you burn it (camp hosts and stores along the way sell it), and expect a California agriculture inspection stop on Hwy 199 that asks about wood and produce.

Stove fuel: Pack all the canisters you need — small coastal stores may not carry your size.
