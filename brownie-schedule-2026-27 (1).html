Brownie Year 1 Plan
Welcome, Brownies!
We are Brownies now — and this year belongs to us.
This is a big year. As Brownies, the girls step into a new level of Girl Scouting — one where their voices lead the way. They'll have more say in what we do, where we go, and how we grow together. From voting on badges to dreaming up our biggest adventures, this troop year is theirs to shape. We can't wait to see what they choose, create, and become. Here's to an incredible year ahead!

Dream Box
This year the girls will visualize troop goals for the year. We will decorate the box for the girls to store notes, drawings, flyers they collect for activities they want to do. We will bring the box to each meeting, and if the girls have an idea, they can add it to the box. Some ideas of what to add: overnight camping trip, waterfall hike, science museum sleepover, beach bonfire, cooking class, kayak, attend a WNBA game, travel somewhere new, see The Nutcracker...
