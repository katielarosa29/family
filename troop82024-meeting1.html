Troop 82024 Brownie Year 1

Meeting 1 — Welcome to Brownies!

75 min  ·  Monday 3:15–4:30 PM  ·  3 activity stations

Opening

3:15
0–15 min
Arrival & Snack

Girls arrive, settle in, and enjoy snack together. Leaders use this window to get the room ready and greet families. Snack kapers handle distribution and will clean up at the end.

Snack + napkins/cups.

3:30
15–25 min
Promise, Law & Kapers

Gather girls into a friendship circle for the opening routine:

  • Girl Scout Promise — recite together with the three-finger sign.
  • Girl Scout Law — recite together; pause on a word or two to ask what they mean (e.g. "What does considerate mean?").
  • Quick welcome — celebrate being Brownies! This is their very first official Brownie meeting.

Promise & Law display or printed cards.

Activity Stations — 3:40–4:10 (30 min, ~10 min per rotation)

Station A
Dream Box Decoration

Girls decorate the outside of their Dream Box with stickers, washi tape, markers, and their name. These boxes hold their hopes and dreams for the year — opened at the end-of-year celebration.

Plain boxes, stickers, markers, foam stickers, etc.

Station B
Dream Box Contents

Girls fill out a "Brownie Dreams" card (or draw a picture) answering: What do you hope to do this year? What badge do you want most? What does being a Brownie mean to you? Card goes inside the box.

Pre-printed dream cards or blank index cards, pencils, colored pencils.

Station C
Rules Activity

Start with a group discussion about why rules exist and what makes a good one. Then use yes/no cards to sort rule scenarios together. Finish with sticky notes — girls write any rules they think are missing and add them to the troop rules poster.

Yes/no cards, scenario cards, sticky notes, markers, troop rules poster.

Rotation: Split girls into 3 groups. Signal rotations with a clap pattern at ~10-minute marks (3:40 → 3:50 → 4:00 → 4:10). Leaders stay at their station; girls rotate.

Closing

4:10
55–65 min
Dream Box seal & share

Gather back together. Girls place their dream card inside their box and close the lid. Go around the circle — each girl shares one Brownie dream from her card. Leaders collect boxes for safekeeping until the end-of-year celebration.

4:20
65–75 min
"Make New Friends" & friendship circle

Form the friendship circle with the pinky squeeze. Sing "Make New Friends" together — do the round if the group is ready. End with the Girl Scout sign. Snack cleanup kapers tidy up. Remind girls of the next meeting date.

🎵 Make new friends, but keep the old,
one is silver and the other gold.
A circle is round, it has no end,
that's how long I want to be your friend.
Leader tip: Print the troop's eight rules and read them aloud together before Station C — it gives the rules activity real context. Station C works best with a leader or parent volunteer facilitating the discussion.
