Ginger Miso Tofu Ramen
Vegetarian · Asian Night · 30 min · Serves 4
Asian NightVegetarianPhytoestrogensKit-friendly
▾
Ingredients
  • 4 portionsramen or udon noodles
  • 14 ozfirm tofu, cubed and crisped
  • 5 cupsvegetable broth
  • 3 tbspwhite miso paste
  • 1 tbspred miso paste
  • 1 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tbspsesame oil
  • 2-inchginger knob, grated
  • 4 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 2 cupsshiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 headsbaby bok choy, halved
  • 4soft-boiled eggs, halved
  • Scallions, nori, sesame seeds, chili oil to finish
Instructions
  1. Press and cube tofu. Pan-fry in 1 tbsp neutral oil over high heat without stirring 3–4 min until golden. Flip and cook 2 more min. Set aside.
  2. In a pot, sauté garlic and ginger in sesame oil 1 min. Add mushrooms and cook 3 min. Add broth and bring to a simmer.
  3. Whisk both misos and soy sauce into a splash of hot broth, then stir back into the pot. Never boil after adding miso — it kills the probiotics and flattens the flavor.
  4. Add bok choy to the broth for the last 2 min. Cook noodles separately, drain.
  5. Build bowls: noodles, ladle broth over, top with crispy tofu, bok choy, soft egg, scallions, nori, sesame seeds. Chili oil for adults.
💡 Miso + tofu = double phytoestrogens (isoflavones) — one of the most studied foods for perimenopause symptom relief. Shiitakes are also one of the few plant sources of vitamin D. Kit loves ramen; this version is genuinely just as satisfying as the pork version.

Lentil Bolognese with Pappardelle
Vegetarian · Italian Night · 45 min · Serves 4–6
Italian NightVegetarianIron-richHigh fiber
▾
Ingredients
  • 1½ cupsgreen or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 12 ozpappardelle or tagliatelle
  • 1 cancrushed tomatoes (28 oz)
  • ½ cupdry red wine
  • 1onion, finely diced
  • 2carrots, finely diced
  • 2 stalkscelery, finely diced
  • 5 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 2 tbsptomato paste
  • 3 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tspdried oregano + thyme
  • 2 cupsvegetable broth
  • Parmesan + fresh basil, to finish
Instructions
  1. Warm olive oil in a large heavy pot. Sauté onion, carrot, and celery over medium heat 8 min (the soffritto — this is the flavor base, take your time).
  2. Add garlic and tomato paste. Cook 2 min until paste darkens slightly.
  3. Add wine and cook until almost evaporated, 3 min.
  4. Add lentils, crushed tomatoes, broth, and herbs. Bring to a simmer. Cook 25–30 min until lentils are tender and sauce is thick. Season generously.
  5. Cook pasta in salted water. Reserve ½ cup pasta water. Toss pasta with sauce, adding pasta water to loosen. Finish with parmesan and basil.
💡 Lentils are one of the richest plant sources of iron AND protein — a major win for perimenopause when iron levels can drop. The red wine deepens the flavor enormously; it cooks off completely. Kit: this tastes just like meat bolognese to kids — serve it the same way. Make a double batch and freeze half.

Paneer Tikka Masala
Vegetarian · Wildcard Night · 40 min · Serves 4
Wildcard NightVegetarianCalciumAnti-inflammatory
▾
Ingredients
  • 14 ozpaneer, cubed
  • ½ cupGreek yogurt
  • 1 tsp eachgaram masala, cumin, turmeric, chili powder
  • — Masala Sauce —
  • 1 cancrushed tomatoes (14 oz)
  • 1large onion, diced
  • 5 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1-inchginger, grated
  • 1 tspKashmiri chili powder or sweet paprika
  • 1 tspgaram masala + cumin + coriander
  • ½ cupheavy cream or coconut cream
  • 1 tspdried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi)
  • 2 tbspneutral oil + 1 tbsp butter
  • Basmati rice + warm naan, to serve
Instructions
  1. Marinate paneer in yogurt and spices for at least 30 min. Thread onto skewers or pan-fry in a cast-iron until charred on all sides, 2–3 min per side. Set aside.
  2. In the same pan, melt butter and oil. Sauté onion 8 min until deep golden. Add garlic, ginger, and dry spices. Cook 2 min until fragrant.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer 10 min until sauce thickens and darkens. Let cool slightly, then blend until silky smooth.
  4. Return sauce to the pan. Stir in cream and kasuri methi. Simmer 5 min. Fold in charred paneer. Taste and season.
  5. Serve over basmati rice with warm naan for scooping.
💡 Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek) is the ingredient that makes tikka masala taste like the restaurant — it has a slightly bitter, maple-like flavor. Find it at any Indian grocery. Turmeric is one of the strongest anti-inflammatory foods you can eat during perimenopause. Blending the sauce is non-negotiable — that silky texture is everything. Kit approved.

Sundubu Jjigae — Korean Soft Tofu Stew
Vegetarian · Asian Night · 20 min · Serves 4
Asian NightVegetarianPhytoestrogens20 minutes
▾
Ingredients
  • 2 tubessoft or silken tofu (12 oz each)
  • 2 tbspgochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes)
  • 1 tbspgochujang paste
  • 4 cupsvegetable broth
  • 1 cupshiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 1zucchini, halved and sliced
  • ½onion, diced
  • 4 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tbspsesame oil
  • 2 tbspsoy sauce
  • 4eggs (one cracked per bowl at the end)
  • Scallions, steamed rice, sesame seeds to serve
Instructions
  1. Heat sesame oil in a wide shallow pot or Korean clay pot (dolsot) over medium heat. Add garlic and onion, cook 2 min.
  2. Add gochugaru and gochujang. Stir for 1 min until fragrant and the oil turns deeply red.
  3. Add mushrooms and zucchini, cook 2 min. Pour in broth and soy sauce. Bring to a boil.
  4. Spoon soft tofu directly into the broth in large chunks — don't break it up too much, you want silky pieces.
  5. Crack one raw egg into each bowl's portion of stew (or into the pot if serving family-style). Cover and cook 1–2 min until the white is just set but yolk is still runny. Serve immediately over steamed rice.
💡 Soft tofu (sundubu) is the single best perimenopause food — it has the highest isoflavone content of any soy product, and research specifically links regular tofu consumption to reduced hot flash frequency and better bone density. The egg cracked on top adds vitamin D and protein. Kit mod: milder — reduce gochugaru by half and skip the gochujang. Gochugaru is sold at Fred Meyer's Asian aisle or H Mart.

Ribollita — Tuscan Kale & White Bean Soup
Vegetarian · Italian Night · 45 min · Serves 6
Italian NightBlue ZoneCalcium (kale)Iron-rich
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 bunchlacinato (dinosaur) kale, stems removed, torn
  • 2 canscannellini beans (15 oz each), drained
  • 3 thick slicesstale sourdough or ciabatta, torn
  • 1 cancrushed tomatoes (14 oz)
  • 1onion, diced
  • 2carrots, diced
  • 2 stalkscelery, diced
  • 5 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 2 sprigsrosemary
  • 4 cupsvegetable broth
  • 4 tbspgood olive oil
  • Parmesan rind + shaved parm to finish
Instructions
  1. Warm 3 tbsp olive oil in a large pot. Sauté onion, carrot, celery 8 min. Add garlic and rosemary, cook 2 min.
  2. Add tomatoes, broth, parmesan rind, and one can of beans (whole). Mash the second can with a fork and stir in — this thickens the soup.
  3. Bring to a simmer. Add kale and cook 15 min until very tender and silky.
  4. Add torn stale bread directly to the soup. Stir until the bread breaks down and thickens everything — this is what "ribollita" means: reboiled, thickened. Simmer 10 more min.
  5. Remove rosemary. Season generously. Serve in deep bowls with a heavy drizzle of your best olive oil and shaved parmesan.
💡 Lacinato kale is one of the richest plant sources of calcium — and significantly more bioavailable than spinach. Combined with white beans (iron + protein + fiber), this is an extraordinary perimenopause meal. The bread is not optional — it's what makes ribollita ribollita. Use truly stale bread; fresh bread turns gluey. Gets even better the next day.

Italian Night Heart healthy Iron-rich Pantry meal

Ingredients
  • 12 ozspaghetti or linguine
  • 2 canstuna in olive oil (5 oz each), drained
  • 4 tbspextra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 clovesgarlic, sliced thin
  • 2 tbspcapers, rinsed
  • 1 cupcherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 cupsbaby spinach
  • ½ tspred pepper flakes
  • Zest + juice of 1 lemon
  • Parmesan + fresh parsley, to finish
  • Kit: pull her portion before adding tuna — butter + parm + spinach
Instructions
  1. Cook pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Warm olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add garlic and red pepper flakes; sauté 2 min until golden at the edges.
  3. Add cherry tomatoes; cook 3–4 min until they begin to burst. Add capers.
  4. For Kit: pull her portion of pasta now, toss with butter, parm, and a handful of wilted spinach.
  5. Add tuna to the pan, breaking into chunks. Add spinach and toss until wilted. Add pasta, tossing with pasta water until sauce coats everything.
  6. Off heat, stir in lemon zest and juice. Finish with parmesan and parsley.
💡 Tuna in olive oil (not water) is the move here — Genova or Ortiz brands are worth it. The pasta water is what makes the sauce silky. Capers add a briny punch that balances the richness; leave them out for Joey if needed.

Pasta e Fagioli
Family recipe · Italian Night · 35 min · Serves 4–6
Italian Night Blue Zone classic Iron-rich Vegetarian
▾
Ingredients
  • 2 canscannellini beans (15 oz each), drained
  • 1 cupditalini or small pasta
  • 1 cancrushed tomatoes (14 oz)
  • 4 cupsvegetable or chicken broth
  • 5 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 2 sprigsfresh rosemary
  • 3 tbspextra-virgin olive oil
  • 1Parmesan rind (optional but excellent)
  • Salt + pepper
  • Parmesan + good olive oil, to finish
Instructions
  1. Warm olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add garlic and rosemary sprigs; cook 2 min until fragrant.
  2. Add crushed tomatoes, cook 3 min. Add broth and parmesan rind if using. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Add 1½ cans of beans whole. Mash the remaining ½ can with a fork and stir in — this thickens the broth beautifully.
  4. Simmer 15 min. Remove rosemary sprigs and parmesan rind.
  5. Add pasta directly to the pot and cook according to package until al dente, stirring often.
  6. Serve immediately with a heavy drizzle of good olive oil and lots of parmesan.
💡 The pasta swells and absorbs liquid as it sits — serve right away or the dish becomes very thick (still good, just different). White beans are excellent for iron and plant protein. This is the meal eaten most in the Blue Zone centenarian villages of Sardinia and Calabria.

Shakshuka with Warm Pita
Family recipe · Wildcard Night · 25 min · Serves 4
Wildcard Night Vegetarian Iron-rich One pan
▾
Ingredients
  • 6large eggs
  • 1 cancrushed tomatoes (28 oz)
  • 1red bell pepper, diced
  • 1onion, diced
  • 4 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspcumin
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • ½ tspred pepper flakes
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • ½ cupcrumbled feta
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro, to finish
  • Warm pita, for serving
Instructions
  1. Warm olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet. Sauté onion and bell pepper over medium heat 5–6 min until soft.
  2. Add garlic, cumin, paprika, and red pepper flakes. Cook 1 min until fragrant.
  3. Pour in crushed tomatoes. Season with salt and simmer 8–10 min until sauce thickens and deepens in color.
  4. Make 6 shallow wells in the sauce with the back of a spoon. Crack an egg into each well.
  5. Cover and cook over medium-low heat 5–7 min until whites are set but yolks are still slightly runny.
  6. Scatter feta and fresh herbs on top. Bring the pan to the table with warm pita.
💡 Eggs set quickly — start checking at 5 min. The yolk should still be a little jammy when you pull it; it continues cooking in the residual heat. Joey mod: skip the red pepper flakes and serve his egg on plain rice if the sauce isn't his thing. Tomatoes are a good iron source and vitamin C boosts absorption.

Kit-friendly Iron-rich Add chicken/shrimp 25 minutes

  • 3 cupscooked rice, day-old if possible

  • 3scallions, sliced
  • 3 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tbspsesame oil
  • 2 tbspneutral oil (avocado or vegetable)
  • Sriracha or chili oil, to finish (adults)
  • Optional: cooked chicken or shrimp for Joey + Justin
Instructions
  1. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add tofu cubes and cook without stirring for 3–4 min until golden and crisp on one side, then toss and cook another 2 min. Remove and set aside.

Homemade Mac & Cheese

Kids love it Stovetop Comfort food
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 lbelbow macaroni or shells
  • 4 tbspbutter
  • ¼ cupall-purpose flour

  • ½ cupheavy cream
  • 2 cupssharp cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cupgruyère or mozzarella, shredded
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • ½ tspgarlic powder
  • ½ tsppaprika
  • Salt + white pepper to taste
Instructions
  1. Cook pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water. Drain.

  1. Cook bacon in a large pot over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside, leaving drippings in pot.

Dessert
Paleo Chocolate Chip Cookies
Bakerita ↗ · ~20 min · Makes ~16 cookies
Paleo Gluten free Dairy free option No one guesses!
▾
Ingredients
  • 2 cupsalmond flour (blanched)
  • ¼ tspbaking soda
  • ¼ tspfine sea salt
  • ¼ cupcoconut oil, melted
  • ¼ cuppure maple syrup
  • 1egg (or flax egg for vegan)
  • 1 tspvanilla extract
  • ¾ cupdark chocolate chips (dairy-free if needed)
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whisk together almond flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix melted coconut oil, maple syrup, egg, and vanilla.
  4. Stir wet into dry until a dough forms. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Scoop ~1.5 tbsp rounds onto parchment, spacing 2 inches apart. Flatten slightly.
  6. Bake 9–11 min until edges are golden. Centers will look underdone — that's right.
  7. Cool on pan 10 min before moving — they firm up as they cool.
💡 Bakerita says "no one guesses these are gluten-free" — and it's true. The key is not overbaking; pull them when edges are just golden. They'll seem too soft but firm up perfectly. Store in fridge up to a week.
