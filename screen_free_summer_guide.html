Summer without screens

Ideas for Joey & Kit — for rest weeks, mornings & in-between days

Joey 3rd grade · age 9
Kit 1st grade · age 7
🌿

Get outside

Nature scavenger hunt

Make a list each morning — bugs, leaves, birds, clouds. First to find all wins.

Backyard obstacle course

Lawn chairs, hula hoops, chalk lines. Time each other. Reset & repeat.

Neighborhood bike ride

Pick a new destination each time — park, bakery, a street you've never tried.

Sprinkler & chalk day

Cover the driveway in chalk art, then run through the sprinkler to "erase" it.

Grow something

Sunflowers, cherry tomatoes, or herbs from seeds. Check progress daily.

Morning hike

Pick a trail, bring snacks, take photos of five cool things you find.

🎨

Make things

Summer scrapbook

Photos, ticket stubs, drawings, pressed leaves. One page per week.

Tie-dye shirts

One shirt per person, one design per camp week — a wearable memory.

Cardboard city

Boxes, tape, paint. Build a town, add roads with chalk outside.

Watercolor station

Set it up on the porch. Paint what you see, what you did, or just anything.

Friendship bracelets

Learn one new knot pattern per week. Make extras for camp friends.

Cook something new

Each kid picks one recipe per week to make together from scratch.

🧠

Think & play

Puzzle challenge

Time yourselves on the same puzzle each week and try to beat the record.

Lego freestyle

No instructions — pick a theme and build. Vote on each other's creation.

Science experiments

Baking soda volcano, slime, invisible ink, or a simple paper-airplane contest.

Summer journal

One sentence or one drawing per day. Read them back at the end of August.

Board game deep dive

Catan, chess, Ticket to Ride — pick one game and really learn it this summer.

Book + hammock hour

Same time each afternoon. No agenda, just reading wherever they want.

🤝

Get social

Lemonade stand

Let the kids handle the whole thing — pricing, signs, making change.

Themed playdate

Spy day, cooking competition, Olympics in the backyard — pick a theme.

Write letters

Pen-pal style to grandparents, cousins, or friends from camp.

Backyard movie night

Outside on a blanket counts as an event, not screen time. Make popcorn.

💡

Tips for making it stick

🗓️

Morning meeting: 5 minutes each day to pick one activity. Giving them ownership means less "I'm bored" by 10am.

📦

Activity bin: Keep supplies (chalk, paint, clay, cards) in one visible bin so the barrier to starting is zero.

⏱️

Time it: Kids respond well to "you have 20 minutes before lunch — what can you build?" Constraints spark creativity.

🏆

Weekly challenge: Post one challenge on the fridge each Monday — most bugs found, best drawing, farthest bike ride.

🤝

Let them teach each other: Joey teaches Kit something from football camp; Kit teaches Joey something from drama. Sibling bonding built in.

"The best summer memories aren't planned — they just need a little space to happen."
