Momofuku Noodle Bowls with Mango & Cucumber
Pantry · ~20 min · Serves 4
Pantry mealQuickCustomizable
▾
Ingredients
  • 2 packsMomofuku noodles
  • 1mango, sliced thin
  • 1cucumber, sliced or ribboned
  • 4soft-boiled eggs (optional)
  • Tofu oredamame for extra protein
  • Sesame oil,soy sauce, lime if on hand
  • Scallions,sesame seeds to top
Instructions
  1. Cook noodles per package. Drain and toss with the included sauce packet.
  2. Divide into bowls. Top with mango slices, cucumber, and a soft-boiled egg if using.

  4. Add tofu or edamame for extra protein — sauté tofu cubes quickly in a hot pan.
🔵 Kit: If the sauce packet is chicken-based, season her bowl with soy sauce + sesame oil instead. She'll love the mango. 🟢 Justin: Skip the egg yolk or use whites only; load up on tofu or edamame for protein.

Spinach Ravioli with Brown Butter & Sage
Pantry · ~20 min · Serves 4
VegetarianQuickSourdough side
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 pkgspinach ravioli
  • 4 tbspbutter
  • 8–10fresh sage leaves (or ½ tsp dried)
  • 2 clovesgarlic, sliced thin
  • Parmesanto finish
  • Salt, pepper,pinch of nutmeg
  • Sourdoughbread, for the side
Instructions
  1. Cook ravioli per package; reserve ¼ cup pasta water.
  2. In a wide pan over medium heat, melt butter. Add sage and garlic. Let butter foam, then turn golden brown and nutty — about 3–4 min. Watch closely.
  3. Add ravioli to the brown butter, toss gently. Add a splash of pasta water to loosen.
  4. Plate, finish with Parmesan, black pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg.
  5. Toast sourdough slices alongside.
🔵 Kit + Katie: Fully vegetarian. 🟢 Justin: Use 2 tbsp butter instead of 4, or swap half for olive oil — same flavor, less sat fat. Plenty of Parmesan on top is fine.

Rice Vermicelli Bowl with Mango, Cucumber & Tofu
Pantry · ~25 min · Serves 4
VegetarianLightFresh
▾
Ingredients
  • Ricevermicelli noodles
  • 1mango, diced or sliced
  • 1cucumber, sliced thin
  • 1 blockfirm tofu, cubed + pan-fried
  • Fresh herbs:mint, cilantro, basil if available
  • Lime juice,fish sauce or soy, honey
  • Sesame seeds,chopped peanuts to top
Instructions
  1. Soak vermicelli in hot water per package (~5 min), drain and rinse with cold water.
  2. Pan-fry tofu cubes in a hot oiled skillet until golden on all sides. Season with soy sauce.
  3. Quick dressing: 2 tbsp lime juice, 1 tbsp soy or fish sauce, 1 tsp honey, ½ tsp sesame oil. Whisk.
  4. Build bowls: noodles, mango, cucumber, crispy tofu, herbs. Drizzle dressing over everything.
  5. Top with sesame seeds and peanuts.
🔵 Kit: She can have hers deconstructed — noodles plain with a little soy sauce, mango on the side, tofu if she'll try it. 🟢 Justin: This is a great low-cholesterol bowl — tofu protein, fresh produce, light dressing. ⭐ Use fish sauce in the dressing for more depth, or keep all soy to stay vegetarian.
🛒 Pantry wins this week — minimal shopping needed. You may want to grab: soft-boiled egg supplies (Mon), fresh sage or dried (Tue), shredded cheese + salsa (Thu), mozzarella (Fri), tofu + fresh herbs (Sat). Check if the Costco curry has meat before Wed.
