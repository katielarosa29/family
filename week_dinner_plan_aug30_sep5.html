SundayAug 30
Family Recipe
Sunday Roast Chicken with Preserved Lemon
▾
Whole chicken stuffed with preserved lemon, garlic, and herbs — rubbed with herbes de Provence and olive oil, roasted on a bed of potatoes, carrots, fennel, and red onion at 425°F for 75 min. Classic Sunday dinner to send the kids into the week on a high.
Kit + Katie: Separate sheet pan with the same roasted veg, a can of white beans, and crumbled feta. Same oven, same time.
Justin: White meat chicken + olive oil base — excellent low-cholesterol Sunday roast. Skip the buttered potatoes, load up on the veg.
Low cholesterol Kit + Katie: veg pan 1 hr 20 min Preserved lemon — Fred Meyer specialty aisle
MondayAug 31
Mediterranean
Miso Salmon Rice Bowls
First Day of School! ▾
Salmon glazed with white miso, soy sauce, mirin, and sesame oil — roasted at 425°F for 12 min. Served over steamed rice with cucumber, avocado, and snap peas. Quick enough for a school night. Celebratory enough for a milestone.
Kit + Katie: Same bowl with crispy tofu — same miso glaze, roasted alongside the salmon. Full meal, identical setup.
Justin: Miso salmon is one of the best heart-healthy dinners around — omega-3s, no saturated fat, and it tastes like a restaurant.
Tip: Make the miso glaze in the morning before school drop-off. Brush on the salmon (or tofu) when you get home — 12 min in the oven, done.
Omega-3s · heart healthy Kit + Katie: tofu version 25 min Celebrate first day of school!
TuesdaySep 1
Italian
Lentil Bolognese with Pappardelle
▾
Green lentils slow-cooked with soffritto (onion, carrot, celery), red wine, crushed tomatoes, and herbs until thick and deeply savory. Tastes so much like meat bolognese that the kids won't notice. Everyone eats the same meal — zero modifications needed.
Kit + Katie: This IS the vegetarian meal. Lentils are the protein for everyone at the table tonight.
Justin: Lentil bolognese has virtually zero saturated fat — high fiber and plant protein, one of the best cholesterol-lowering pasta nights possible.
Low cholesterol · high fiber Everyone eats the same 45 min Iron-rich — great for Kit
WednesdaySep 2
Brinner Night
High-Protein Blender Pancakes + Scrambled Eggs
Literary Arts 7pm ▾
The blender pancake recipe from the file — Fairlife milk, Clean Simple Eats vanilla protein powder, 6 eggs, baked at 350°F for 15–20 min. Plus scrambled eggs on the side. Full brinner spread on the table in 25 minutes.
Kit + Katie: Fully vegetarian — pancakes and eggs are both on the menu. Perfect meal for Kit's protein and iron goals.
Justin: Eggs are moderate-cholesterol — fine occasionally, especially with a high-protein payoff. Keep the syrup light.
Timing: Literary Arts is at 7pm. Mix the batter at 5:45, bake at 6:00, on the table by 6:20. You've got plenty of time.
High protein Kit + Katie: veg 25 min Must eat by 6:30pm
ThursdaySep 3
Mexican
Fish Tacos with Mango Salsa
▾
Grilled cod or tilapia with chili powder and cumin. Topped with fresh mango salsa (mango, red onion, cilantro, lime), crunchy cabbage slaw, and a Greek yogurt crema. Build-your-own taco night — everyone loads their own at the table.
Kit + Katie: Same taco setup with grilled halloumi instead of fish — sear 2–3 min per side in a dry pan until golden. The mango salsa is the star for everyone.
Justin: White fish is one of the lowest-cholesterol proteins you can eat. Greek yogurt crema instead of sour cream keeps it clean.
Low cholesterol · omega-3s Kit + Katie: halloumi 20 min Build your own
FridaySep 4
Pizza Night
FAIS Picnic = Dinner · Optional flatbread after
FAIS Picnic 5pm ▾
The Back-to-School Picnic starts at 5pm — that's dinner time. Don't double-cook. Enjoy it, let the kids run around with their new classmates, and if anyone's still hungry after, flatbreads take 10 minutes.
Kit + Katie: Mediterranean flatbread — olive oil, tomatoes, mozzarella, fresh basil. Fully vegetarian and done in 10 min if needed.
Justin: Olive oil base, light on the cheese — heart-healthy flatbread if the picnic wasn't enough.
Tip: Keep flatbreads, mozzarella, and cherry tomatoes stocked. If you need them, you've got a 10-min backup. If you don't, enjoy the free Friday night.
Picnic IS dinner 10 min backup only Free Friday night
SaturdaySep 5
Healthy Bowl
Café Yumm Bowls
▾
Brown rice, warm black beans, sliced avocado, cherry tomatoes, shredded cheddar, olives, sour cream, cilantro, and fresh salsa — topped with a generous pour of homemade Yumm Sauce. Make the sauce on Sunday or Thursday so Saturday is pure assembly.
Kit + Katie: This is fully vegetarian as-is — everyone eats the same bowl. High protein from beans, great iron from the black beans + rice combo.
Justin: Plant-based Yumm Sauce (chickpeas, almonds, nutritional yeast, lemon, olive oil), beans for fiber — excellent cholesterol profile.
Make ahead: Yumm Sauce keeps in the fridge all week. If you make it Sunday after the roast chicken, Saturday is pure assembly — 10 min max.
Plant-based · cholesterol friendly Everyone eats the same 10 min assembly if sauce is made Make Yumm Sauce on Sunday!
