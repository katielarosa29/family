Beefy Skillet Lasagna

All the cozy flavor of lasagna, no layering required — everything cooks in one pan.

⏱ ~30 min 🍳 One pan 🧒 Kid approved
Servings
4
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1 lbground beef
  • ½yellow onion, diced
  • 2garlic cloves, minced
  • 24 ozjarred marinara sauce
  • 1½ cupswater or chicken broth
  • 8lasagna noodles, broken into pieces
  • 1 tspItalian seasoning
  • ½ tspsalt
  • ¼ tspblack pepper
  • ¾ cupplain whole-milk Greek yogurt (swapped for ricotta)
  • 1½ cupsshredded mozzarella
  • ¼ cupparmesan, grated (plus extra for the yogurt)
  • 2 tbspfresh basil or parsley, chopped (optional)
🥄 Yogurt swap: stir a spoonful of parmesan into the Greek yogurt before dolloping it on — rounds out the tang so it reads more like a cheese filling. Add off the heat so it doesn't split.
👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. Brown the beef
    In a large deep skillet or pot, brown the ground beef with onion and garlic over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks, about 6–8 minutes until no longer pink. Drain excess grease if needed.
    ⏱ 8 min
  2. Add sauce and liquid
    Stir in marinara, water or broth, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Bring to a simmer.
  3. Cook the noodles in the sauce
    Add the broken lasagna noodles directly into the simmering sauce, stirring to submerge them. Cover and simmer, stirring occasionally so they don't stick, until noodles are tender, about 12–15 minutes.
    ⏱ 15 min
  4. Add the cheese
    Reduce heat to low. Dollop spoonfuls of the parmesan-stirred yogurt over the top, then sprinkle with mozzarella and parmesan. Cover and let sit off heat (or on very low heat) for 3–5 minutes until cheese is melted.
    ⏱ 5 min
  5. Serve
    Garnish with fresh basil or parsley if using. Serve hot straight from the pan.
💡 Kid tip: this is endlessly customizable — leave out the yogurt dollops if your kids prefer it more "saucy," or stir the cheese in completely instead of leaving pockets. Leftovers reheat great with a splash of water to loosen the sauce.
