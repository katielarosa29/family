His plan follows the same structure, with two adjustments for jujutsu training: Wednesday and Friday he fasts overnight and through the morning, eats at 8am right after training, then eats normally through the day.

Practical guidance to make the week feel easy and sustainable — not like a diet.

Hydration
2–3L water daily. Black coffee, plain green tea, and sparkling water are fine during fasts. Avoid diet drinks — artificial sweeteners may trigger an insulin response.
Electrolytes
During extended fasts, add a pinch of salt to water. Most fasting headaches are electrolyte depletion, not hunger. Bone broth also works.
Sleep
Aim for 7–8 hours nightly. Growth hormone peaks during sleep and synergizes with fasting for cellular repair. Sleep counts as fasting time.
Exercise
Light resistance exercise on regular days amplifies AMPK activation. Avoid intense workouts during the 24h and 36h extended fasts.
Flexibility
The eating window can shift (10am–6pm vs noon–8pm) when social meals require it. Consistency matters more than precision.
Weight loss
Fasting naturally reduces incidental calories without counting. Don't restrict food in the eating window — eat well and enjoy it. The reduction happens automatically.
Coffee & tea
Black coffee and plain green tea both extend autophagy signaling and are fine during any fast. They can help with hunger in the morning window.
When to stop
Heart palpitations, confusion, or dizziness that electrolytes don't fix = break the fast immediately. Hunger, mild fatigue, and headache are normal and expected.
For general wellness. Consult your doctor before beginning if you have a medical condition.
