Meal Plan — Veggie Edition

Dinners, camp lunches, and snacks — built around Kit's new vegetarian plate, with easy swaps so nobody feels singled out.

🍽️ Dinners
Mon
All-veggie night
Black Bean & Sweet Potato Quesadillas
Whole family veggie ~$1.50/serving
On the plate
  • Mashed roasted sweet potato + black beans
  • Shredded cheese, folded in tortillas
  • Pan-fried until crispy
Toppings
  • Greek yogurt (sour cream swap)
  • Mild salsa
  • Avocado slices
🌱 Budget-friendly and naturally kid-loved — quesadillas are already a familiar format. Beans + cheese cover protein easily.
Tue
Combo night
Sheet Pan Chicken Fajitas
Combo — veggie swap for Kit ⏱ 30 min
On the pan
  • Chicken strips, bell peppers, onion
  • Tossed with oil + taco seasoning + lime
  • Roast at 425°F, 18–20 min
Kit's veggie swap
  • Black beans roasted alongside in a corner of the pan, same seasoning
  • Same peppers + onions + toppings bar for everyone
🫑 One pan does it all — just section off a corner for seasoned black beans instead of chicken. Same toppings bar means nobody's meal looks different from across the table.
Wed
All-veggie night
Chickpea Tomato Skillet over Rice
Whole family veggie ~$1.25/serving
In the skillet
  • Canned chickpeas, canned tomatoes
  • Garlic, cumin, paprika
  • Simmered until thick
Serve with
  • Rice or warm pita
  • Shredded cheese on top
  • Plain yogurt dollop
🌱 One of the cheapest dinners you can make — canned chickpeas + tomatoes cost very little and reheat great for lunch leftovers too.
Thu
Combo night
Mac & Cheese — Add Chicken Sausage / Plain for Kit
Combo — easy swap ~$1.50/serving
Base for everyone
  • Stovetop mac & cheese (5-ingredient version)
  • Frozen peas stirred in for everyone
Family add-on
  • Sliced chicken sausage, pan-browned
  • Stirred into adult/Joey bowls only — Kit's bowl stays meat-free
🧀 Same pot, same base — just hold back Kit's portion before adding the sausage. Zero extra dishes.
Fri
All-veggie night
Sheet Pan Veggie Frittata + Pancakes
Whole family veggie ~$1.75/serving
On the sheet pan
  • Roast veggies first (peppers, broccoli, tomatoes)
  • Pour beaten eggs + cheese over, bake until set
Side
  • Whole-grain pancakes (breakfast-for-dinner combo!)
  • Maple syrup, butter
🍳 Breakfast-for-dinner is always a hit and naturally vegetarian — easy Friday night win with minimal cleanup.
