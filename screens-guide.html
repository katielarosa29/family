ADHD Parent Guide

Screens & kids who love them 📱

The honest headline
A thriving kid who loves screens in their downtime is not a problem. Screens are neurologically irresistible to ADHD brains — they deliver constant novelty, immediate feedback, and instant reward. That pull is real and it makes sense. This guide isn't about eliminating screens. It's about understanding why they're so appealing, what actually matters to watch for, and how to build a family approach that doesn't make screens more powerful by making them forbidden.
