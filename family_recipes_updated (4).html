Tofu & Veggie Fried Rice
Family recipe · One pan · 25 min · Serves 4
Kit-friendly Iron-rich Add chicken/shrimp 25 minutes
▾
Ingredients
  • 14 ozfirm tofu, pressed and cubed
  • 3 cupscooked rice, day-old if possible
  • 3eggs, beaten
  • 1 cupfrozen peas and carrots
  • 3scallions, sliced
  • 3 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspfresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbspsoy sauce
  • 1 tbspsesame oil
  • 2 tbspneutral oil (avocado or vegetable)
  • Sriracha or chili oil, to finish (adults)
  • Optional: cooked chicken or shrimp for Joey + Justin
Instructions
  1. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Add tofu cubes and cook without stirring for 3–4 min until golden and crisp on one side, then toss and cook another 2 min. Remove and set aside.
  2. Add remaining oil to the pan. Sauté garlic and ginger for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add peas and carrots, cook 2 min. Push everything to the side and scramble the eggs in the empty space, then fold into the veg.
  4. Add the cold rice, breaking up any clumps. Press it against the pan and let it sit 1–2 min to get some crispy bits, then stir.
  5. Pour in soy sauce and sesame oil, toss everything to coat. Fold in the crispy tofu and scallions.
  6. Serve as-is for Kit; add cooked chicken or shrimp to the rest of the pan for Joey and Justin.
💡 Day-old rice is key — fresh rice is too wet and steams instead of frying. Great iron sources here: tofu, eggs, and peas all contribute. A squeeze of lemon or orange over the bowl helps everyone absorb the iron better.

Spinach Pasta — Two Ways
Family recipe · Stovetop · 30 min · Serves 4
Kit: butter & parm Joey + Justin: turkey sauce Iron-rich One pot of pasta
▾
Ingredients
  • 12 ozpasta (spaghetti or penne)
  • 4 cupsfresh baby spinach
  • 3 tbspbutter
  • ½ cupparmesan, grated
  • 2 clovesgarlic, minced
  • Salt + pepperto taste
  • — Turkey Meat Sauce —
  • 1 lbground turkey
  • 24 ozjarred marinara
  • ½onion, diced
  • 2 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspItalian seasoning
  • 1 tbspolive oil
Instructions
  1. Start the turkey sauce first: heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Sauté onion 3–4 min, add garlic 1 min, then add turkey and break it up until cooked through. Stir in marinara and Italian seasoning. Simmer on low while pasta cooks.
  2. Cook pasta in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
  3. In the same pot, melt butter over medium-low. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds. Add spinach and toss until just wilted, 1–2 min.
  4. Add pasta back in and toss with the spinach, butter, and parmesan. Loosen with pasta water as needed. Season well.
  5. Serve Kit a bowl straight from the pot. Ladle turkey meat sauce over the rest for Joey and Justin.
💡 Wilting spinach into the pasta rather than serving it on top is the move — Kit gets the iron without even noticing it. Ground turkey keeps the sauce lean and heart-healthy for Justin. Pair with a glass of OJ or bell pepper strips on the side to maximize iron absorption.

Cheeseburger Stuffed Sweet Potatoes
@eatinghealthyfeed · Oven · 45 min · Serves 6 · 540 cal · 32g protein
Ground beef Sweet potato Kid approved High protein
▾
Ingredients
  • 3 mediumsweet potatoes, halved
  • ½ lbground beef
  • ½ cupshredded cheddar
  • ¼ cupburger sauce (or mixed mayo + ketchup)
  • 1dill pickle, sliced
  • ½onion, diced
  • 1 tsppaprika, salt and pepper
  • Chives, to finish
Instructions
  1. Roast the sweet potato halves cut-side down at 425°F for about 30 minutes, until soft all the way through.
  2. Brown the ground beef with the onion and paprika over medium-high heat until crisp and cooked through.
  3. Scoop a little flesh from the center of each half to make room, then mash it back into the beef for extra body.
  4. Stuff the halves with the beef mixture, top with cheddar, and return to the oven until the cheese melts.
  5. Finish with a drizzle of burger sauce, pickle slices, and a scatter of chives.
💡 Roast the sweet potatoes until a knife slides through easily — undercooked centers won't scoop or hold the filling. Crisp the beef well before stuffing; that browned edge is what gives it the real burger flavor.

Mexican Street Corn Chicken Crunch Bowl
@eatinghealthyfeed · Stovetop · 25 min · Serves 2 · 510 cal · 38g protein
Chicken Rice bowl 25 minutes High protein
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 largechicken breast, sliced
  • 1 cupcooked white rice
  • ½ cupcorn kernels
  • ½avocado, diced
  • 2 tbspcotija, crumbled
  • 1 tbspmayo or Greek yogurt
  • 1 tspchili powder, salt and pepper
  • Cilantro + lime wedges, to finish
Instructions
  1. Season the chicken with chili powder, salt, and pepper, then grill or sear until golden and cooked through.
  2. Char the corn in a hot dry pan, then toss it with the mayo and a little cotija for that classic street-corn finish.
  3. Rest the chicken a couple of minutes before slicing so the juices stay in the meat.

  5. Finish with the remaining cotija, torn cilantro, and lime wedges to squeeze over.
💡 Char the corn in a dry hot pan until it picks up dark spots — that blistering is where the street-corn flavor lives. Season the chicken before it hits the pan so the chili powder toasts into a crust instead of sitting raw on top.

Gochujang Gnocchi & Garlic Beef
@eatinghealthyfeed · Stovetop · 25 min · Serves 4 · 560 cal · 30g protein
Gnocchi Ground beef Spicy 25 minutes
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 lbpotato gnocchi
  • ½ lbground beef
  • 2 tbspgochujang
  • ½ cupcream or half-and-half
  • ¼ cupgrated parmesan
  • 3 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1 tspsoy sauce
  • Scallions + red pepper flakes, to finish
Instructions
  1. Brown the ground beef with the garlic over medium-high heat until deeply crisp, then set a portion aside for topping.
  2. Stir the gochujang and soy sauce into the beef and let it cook for a minute so the paste mellows and deepens.
  3. Pour in the cream and parmesan, stirring into a smooth, glossy sauce.
  4. Boil the gnocchi until they float to the top, then drain and fold them straight into the sauce.
  5. Toss everything to coat, then top with the reserved crispy beef, scallions, and a pinch of red pepper flakes.
💡 Crisp the beef hard and set some aside for the top — that crunchy contrast against the creamy sauce is what makes the dish. Cook the gnocchi just until they float; a minute too long turns them soft and gummy in the sauce.

No cook Vegetarian 15 minutes High protein

  • ¼ cupcanned artichoke hearts, roughly chopped
  • 2 tbspchopped dried cranberries or cherries
  • ¼ cupcrumbled feta cheese
  • ¼ cuploosely packed parsley, minced

Beefy Skillet Lasagna
One pan · ~30 min · Serves 4
Ground beef One pan 30 minutes Kid approved
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 lbground beef
  • ½yellow onion, diced
  • 2garlic cloves, minced
  • 24 ozjarred marinara sauce
  • 1½ cupswater or chicken broth
  • 8lasagna noodles, broken into pieces
  • 1 tspItalian seasoning
  • ½ tspsalt
  • ¼ tspblack pepper
  • ¾ cupplain whole-milk Greek yogurt (swapped for ricotta)
  • 1½ cupsshredded mozzarella
  • ¼ cupparmesan, grated (plus extra for the yogurt)
  • 2 tbspfresh basil or parsley, chopped (optional)
Instructions
  1. Brown the ground beef with onion and garlic over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks, about 6–8 minutes until no longer pink. Drain excess grease if needed.
  2. Stir in marinara, water or broth, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Bring to a simmer.
  3. Add the broken lasagna noodles directly into the simmering sauce, stirring to submerge them. Cover and simmer, stirring occasionally, until noodles are tender, about 12–15 min.
  4. Reduce heat to low. Dollop spoonfuls of the parmesan-stirred yogurt over the top, then sprinkle with mozzarella and parmesan. Cover and let sit 3–5 min until melted.
  5. Garnish with basil or parsley if using. Serve hot straight from the pan.
💡 Yogurt swap: stir a spoonful of parmesan into the Greek yogurt before dolloping it on — rounds out the tang so it reads more like a cheese filling. Add off the heat so it doesn't split. Leave out the yogurt dollops entirely if your kids prefer it more "saucy."

TMP Mini Italian-Style Meatloaf
The Modern Proper ↗ · Sheet pan · ~30 min · Serves 4
Ground beef + pork Sheet pan dinner Freezer friendly Kid approved
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 lbground beef (80/20)
  • ½ lbground pork
  • ½ cupbreadcrumbs
  • 2eggs
  • ¼ cupmilk
  • 1 tspItalian seasoning
  • 1 tspgarlic powder
  • 1 tsponion powder
  • ½ tspsalt + pepper
  • 4 tbspketchup, divided
  • 1 tbspbrown sugar
  • 1 tspWorcestershire sauce
  • Small potatoes + broccoli (for sheet pan)
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a large rimmed sheet pan.
  2. Combine beef, pork, and breadcrumbs in a large bowl.
  3. Whisk eggs, milk, salt, Italian seasoning, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder. Pour into meat with 3 tbsp ketchup. Mix with hands.
  4. Form into 6–8 mini loaves and place on pan.
  5. Mix remaining ketchup, brown sugar, Worcestershire + 1 tsp water. Spread on top of loaves.
  6. Add potatoes and broccoli around loaves with olive oil and salt.
  7. Bake 25–30 min until internal temp hits 165°F.
💡 TMP tip: mini loaves cook much faster than a full meatloaf — check temp at 25 min. These freeze beautifully. Double batch and freeze half for a future easy weeknight dinner.

Homemade Mac & Cheese
Family classic · Stovetop · ~25 min · Serves 6
Kids love it Stovetop Comfort food
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 lbelbow macaroni or shells
  • 4 tbspbutter
  • ¼ cupall-purpose flour
  • 2½ cupswhole milk, warmed
  • ½ cupheavy cream
  • 2 cupssharp cheddar, shredded
  • 1 cupgruyère or mozzarella, shredded
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • ½ tspgarlic powder
  • ½ tsppaprika
  • Salt + white pepper to taste
Instructions
  1. Cook pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water. Drain.
  2. Melt butter in the same pot over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook 1–2 min until golden.
  3. Slowly whisk in warm milk and cream. Cook, stirring, until thickened — about 4 min.
  4. Remove from heat. Stir in Dijon, garlic powder, paprika, salt + pepper.
  5. Add cheese in handfuls, stirring until melted and smooth.
  6. Fold in drained pasta. Loosen with pasta water if needed.
  7. Serve immediately, or pour into a buttered baking dish, top with breadcrumbs and extra cheese, and broil 3–4 min until golden.
💡 Always shred your own cheese — pre-shredded has anti-caking agents that make the sauce grainy. Gruyère gives it a grown-up depth; mozzarella keeps it kid-friendly and stretchy. Both work.

Best Stovetop Mac & Cheese
The Kitchn ↗ · Stovetop · 20 min · Serves 4 · Just 5 ingredients
5 ingredients No roux Kid favorite 20 minutes
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 lbelbow macaroni
  • 3 tbspunsalted butter
  • 3 tbspall-purpose flour
  • 3 cupswhole milk, warmed
  • 3 cupssharp cheddar, freshly shredded
  • Salt + black pepper to taste
Instructions
  1. Cook macaroni in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Melt butter in the same pot over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook 1–2 min until lightly golden.
  3. Slowly whisk in warm milk. Cook, stirring constantly, until thickened — about 4–5 min.
  4. Remove from heat. Add cheese in handfuls, stirring until completely smooth.
  5. Season well with salt and pepper. Fold in drained pasta.
  6. Loosen with reserved pasta water as needed. Serve immediately.
💡 The Kitchn's clever trick: always use freshly shredded cheese — pre-shredded bags contain anti-caking starch that makes the sauce grainy. Warming the milk first prevents lumps. This is the recipe that "tastes like childhood."

Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Vinaigrette
Family recipe · Blender · 5 min · Makes ~1 cup
5 minutes Salad dressing Gluten free
▾
Ingredients
  • ¼ cupoil-packed sun-dried tomatoes
  • ¼ cupmayonnaise or Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbspred wine vinegar
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • 1 smallgarlic clove
  • ⅓ cupolive oil
  • 2–3 tbspwater (to thin)
  • Salt + black pepper to taste
Instructions
  1. Add sun-dried tomatoes, mayo (or Greek yogurt), red wine vinegar, Dijon, and garlic to a blender or food processor.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. With blender running, slowly drizzle in olive oil until emulsified.
  4. Add water 1 tbsp at a time to reach desired consistency.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Taste and adjust.
  6. Store in a jar in the fridge up to 1 week.
💡 Greek yogurt makes it tangier and lighter; mayo makes it richer and creamier. Great on salads, grain bowls, roasted veggies, or as a dip for crusty bread.

Romesco Sauce
Bon Appétit ↗ · Food processor · 10 min · Makes ~1½ cups
Roasted red pepper Almonds Gluten free Great on everything
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 largeroasted red bell pepper (from jar)
  • 1garlic clove, smashed
  • ½ cupslivered almonds, toasted
  • ¼ cuptomato purée
  • 2 tbspchopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 2 tbspsherry vinegar
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • ½ tspcayenne pepper
  • ½ cupextra-virgin olive oil
  • Fine sea salt + freshly ground black pepper
Instructions
  1. Pulse roasted pepper, garlic, almonds, tomato purée, parsley, sherry vinegar, smoked paprika, and cayenne in a food processor until very finely chopped.
  2. With motor running, slowly drizzle in olive oil until emulsified and smooth.
  3. Season generously with salt and pepper. Taste and adjust.
  4. Store in an airtight jar in the fridge up to 5 days.
💡 Incredible on grilled chicken, turkey sandwiches, roasted veggies, or simply spread on crusty bread. The smoked paprika is key — don't skip it. Use jarred roasted peppers for ease.

Whole30 Zuppa Toscana
40 Aprons ↗ · One pot · ~35 min · Serves 6
Whole30 Dairy free Gluten free Italian sausage
▾
Ingredients
  • 1 lbItalian sausage, casings removed
  • 4 stripsbacon, chopped
  • 1yellow onion, diced
  • 4 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 3–4medium Yukon Gold potatoes, sliced thin
  • 4 cupschicken broth
  • 2 cupswater
  • 1 canfull-fat coconut milk (13.5 oz)
  • 3 cupskale, stems removed, chopped
  • Red pepper flakes, salt + pepper
Instructions
  1. Cook bacon in a large pot over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside, leaving drippings in pot.
  2. Brown sausage in the same pot, breaking it up. Remove and set aside.
  3. Sauté onion in the drippings until softened, ~4 min. Add garlic and cook 1 min more.
  4. Add potatoes, chicken broth, and water. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer ~10 min until potatoes are tender.
  5. Stir in coconut milk, sausage, and bacon. Season with red pepper flakes, salt + pepper.
  6. Add kale and simmer 3–5 min until wilted.
  7. Taste, adjust seasoning, and serve hot.
💡 Coconut milk replaces the heavy cream for a Whole30-compliant version — you genuinely can't tell the difference once the soup is seasoned. Full-fat coconut milk only; light will make it watery.

Paleo Chocolate Chip Cookies
Bakerita ↗ · ~20 min · Makes ~16 cookies
Paleo Gluten free Dairy free option No one guesses!
▾
Ingredients
  • 2 cupsalmond flour (blanched)
  • ¼ tspbaking soda
  • ¼ tspfine sea salt
  • ¼ cupcoconut oil, melted
  • ¼ cuppure maple syrup
  • 1egg (or flax egg for vegan)
  • 1 tspvanilla extract
  • ¾ cupdark chocolate chips (dairy-free if needed)
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whisk together almond flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix melted coconut oil, maple syrup, egg, and vanilla.
  4. Stir wet into dry until a dough forms. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Scoop ~1.5 tbsp rounds onto parchment, spacing 2 inches apart. Flatten slightly.
  6. Bake 9–11 min until edges are golden. Centers will look underdone — that's right.
  7. Cool on pan 10 min before moving — they firm up as they cool.
💡 Bakerita says "no one guesses these are gluten-free" — and it's true. The key is not overbaking; pull them when edges are just golden. They'll seem too soft but firm up perfectly. Store in fridge up to a week.
