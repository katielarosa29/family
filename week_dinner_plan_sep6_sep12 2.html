SundaySep 6
Family Recipe
Mushroom & Lentil Shepherd's Pie
▾
Green lentils and cremini mushrooms slow-cooked with carrots, celery, rosemary, and a splash of Worcestershire, topped with creamy parmesan-laced mashed potatoes and baked until the top is golden and crispy. Proper Sunday comfort food.
Kit + Katie: Fully vegetarian — lentils and mushrooms are everyone's protein tonight. No modifications needed.
Justin: UV-exposed mushrooms provide vitamin D. Lentils are outstanding for cholesterol — high fiber, high plant protein, zero saturated fat in the filling.
Tip: Make the Yumm Sauce today too while you're in the kitchen — it takes 10 min and Wednesday becomes pure assembly.
Low cholesterol Everyone eats the same Vitamin D · iron 60 min Make Yumm Sauce today!
MondaySep 7
Asian
Thai Fresh Salad Rolls with Peanut Sauce
▾
Rice paper wrappers filled with rice vermicelli, cucumber, carrot, avocado, fresh mango, mint, and basil — served with a ginger-lime peanut dipping sauce. No cooking, no heat, everyone rolls their own at the table. Perfect relaxed Labor Day dinner.
Kit + Katie: Tofu instead of shrimp — same fillings, same peanut sauce. Kit can build her own rolls which makes it way more fun for her.
Justin: Shrimp is very low cholesterol and high protein. Peanut sauce is olive-oil adjacent in fat profile — all healthy fats.
Tip: Soak rice paper 10 seconds only — it keeps softening as you roll. Set everything out assembly-line style and let everyone build their own. Best Labor Day dinner activity.
No cooking · heart healthy Kit: tofu version 30 min prep · no heat Fresh mango + peanut sauce = 🤌
TuesdaySep 8
Italian
Ribollita — Tuscan Kale & White Bean Soup
▾
Lacinato kale, cannellini beans, and stale bread slow-cooked together in a rosemary-garlic tomato broth until thick and deeply comforting. A Blue Zone centenarian staple from the mountains of Tuscany. Gets even better the next day.
Kit + Katie: Fully vegetarian. Lacinato kale is one of the richest plant calcium sources — more bioavailable than spinach.
Justin: White beans + kale + olive oil = outstanding cholesterol profile. High fiber supports LDL reduction.
Bread tip: Leave a loaf out overnight on Monday so it's genuinely stale by Tuesday. Stale bread is what makes ribollita thick and silky — fresh bread turns gluey.
Blue Zone · low cholesterol Everyone eats the same Calcium (kale) · iron 45 min
WednesdaySep 9
Wildcard
Café Yumm Bowls
▾
Brown rice, warm black beans, avocado, cherry tomatoes, shredded cheddar, Kalamata olives, sour cream, fresh cilantro, and salsa — all topped with a generous pour of the Yumm Sauce you made on Sunday. Pure assembly tonight.
Kit + Katie: Fully vegetarian — everyone eats the same bowl. Black beans are a great iron source for Kit.
Justin: Plant-based Yumm Sauce (chickpeas, almonds, nutritional yeast, lemon, olive oil) + black beans + avocado = excellent cholesterol meal.
Tonight is 10 min if the Yumm Sauce is made and rice is cooked. Start the rice when you walk in and dinner is on the table before anyone notices it started.
Plant-based · cholesterol friendly Everyone eats the same 10 min (sauce made ahead) Iron-rich (black beans)
ThursdaySep 10
Mexican
Sweet Potato & Black Bean Enchiladas
▾
Sweet potato and black beans roasted with cumin and smoked paprika, rolled in corn tortillas, smothered in enchilada sauce and melted Monterey Jack. Kit's cheese-only section bakes right in the same dish — one pan, everyone happy.
Kit + Katie: Katie eats the full enchilada. Kit gets a cheese-only section in the same baking dish — same sauce, same melted cheese on top.
Justin: Sweet potatoes for magnesium, black beans for iron and plant protein. No meat, no saturated fat — great Thursday night.
Low cholesterol · magnesium Kit: cheese section same pan Iron-rich (black beans) 45 min
FridaySep 11
Pizza
Butternut Squash, Sage & Ricotta Pizza
▾
Olive oil base with roasted garlic, whole-milk ricotta mixed with nutmeg spread across the dough, caramelized butternut squash, mozzarella, and fresh sage leaves that crisp beautifully in the oven. Finished with a drizzle of honey and red pepper flakes.
Kit + Katie: Fully vegetarian — the sweetness from the squash makes this very kid-friendly. Kit will love it.
Justin: Ricotta is lower in saturated fat than mozzarella. Olive oil base, lots of vegetable — the healthiest pizza on the rotation.
Roast the squash ahead while making enchiladas Thursday — it stores in the fridge and makes Friday a 15-min dinner.
Lower sat fat Fully vegetarian 35 min · 15 min if squash pre-roasted Calcium · magnesium (squash)
SaturdaySep 12
Healthy Bowl
Roasted Beet, Walnut & Farro Bowl
▾
Farro base with roasted beet wedges, toasted walnuts, arugula, fresh orange segments, pickled red onion, and crumbled goat cheese — dressed with a bright lemon-Dijon vinaigrette. Stunning to look at and one of the best perimenopause meals on the list.
Kit + Katie: Kit gets white rice base instead of farro, extra goat cheese, arugula on the side (it's bitter). The beets and oranges she'll enjoy.
Justin: Walnuts are specifically linked to reduced cardiovascular risk. Beets support blood pressure regulation. Olive oil dressing — excellent cholesterol profile.
Roast beets ahead: Wrap whole beets in foil and roast while making Thursday's enchiladas. They keep in the fridge 3–4 days and make Saturday pure assembly.
Omega-3s (walnuts) · low cholesterol Kit: rice base + goat cheese Perimenopause friendly · iron 40 min · less if beets pre-roasted
