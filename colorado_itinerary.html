OPTION A - Fanny's pick
Anything Fanny recommends
Option B — Black Canyon
of the Gunnison · ~3.5 hrs
One of Colorado's most dramatic and underrated parks. Canyon walls nearly 3,000 ft deep, far less crowded than other national parks.
Dogs allowed on rim trails + campgrounds
South Rim Campground (van hookups) or Hipcamp nearby
Stop in Crested Butte on the way
Option C — Indian Peaks
+ Nederland · 30 min away
The close-to-home option — alpine lakes, wildflowers in peak August bloom, and no long driving days. Up Boulder Canyon.
Dogs on leash in most of the wilderness area
Free dispersed camping in Roosevelt National Forest
Nederland has great coffee + quirky mountain town energy
Option D — Buena Vista
+ Collegiate Peaks · ~2.5 hrs
A sweet spot between drive time and scenery. Surrounded by more 14ers than anywhere in the US, with the Arkansas River running through town.
Dog-friendly trails throughout Collegiate Peaks Wilderness
Tons of National Forest dispersed camping nearby
Kids can do a mellow float or wade on the Arkansas River
Option E — Great Sand Dunes
+ Collegiate Peaks · ~2.5 hrs
Colorado's wildest surprise — the tallest sand dunes in North America rising out of the mountains. Kids can run wild, sled down the dunes, and splash in Medano Creek. Dogs are allowed on the dunes and in the park on leash. Nothing else like it in the country.
Dogs allowed on dunes + throughout park
Pinon Flats Campground inside the park, or dog-friendly private sites 30 min away with dark skies and disc golf
Hit the dunes early — afternoon sand gets extremely hot for paws
